# Code of Conduct (Contributor Covenant v2.1, abridged)

## Pledge

We pledge harassment-free participation for everyone, regardless of background.

## Standards

- Use welcoming language; respect viewpoints; accept feedback gracefully
- No harassment, trolling, sexualized content, or personal attacks

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to kv-creates@github.com. Maintainers may warn or ban contributors.

Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
